Direction B: Study journal with marginalia

A clean page with a wide margin column, the way a student annotates a notebook. The number that matters is written large in the margin next to what it describes: cards due beside each deck, the answer keys beside quiz options, the section number beside the reading, the week's figures beside Stats. Rules sit only under list rows; the reading column stays plain. Ink is fountain-pen blue-black; the highlighter is a marker stroke under the words. Dark is the same journal at night: warm charcoal paper, pale ink, an amber marker.

Honest risks

  • The margin column is real markup work: every row needs a margin cell, which touches more JSX than A (still presentational, no logic).
  • The margin takes 52px at 380px wide: body text gets 300px. That's fine for UI rows; the reader keeps the margin narrow and the text plain to compensate.
  • Fewer rules means the notebook reads more quietly than in A; the margin and the marker carry the identity.
  • Dark mode's primary button is pale ink on charcoal: strong and not periwinkle, but it's the brightest thing on the screen, so use one per screen.
