- Focus ring: base.css `:focus-visible` forced `border-radius: 4px`, so `.quiz-opt` lost its 10px corners on focus. (fixed in the redesign: the ring follows each element's own corners)
- Wrap `.quiz-opt:hover` in `@media (hover: hover)` so it doesn't stick on touch screens. (fixed in 7A)
- quiz.css and flashcards.css hardcoded radii (4px, 10px, 14px) and the card shadow `rgb(14 17 38 / .08)` instead of tokens. (fixed in the redesign: `--card-shadow`, radii mapped to `--r-sm` / `--r-lg`)
- Announce the question's position ("Question 3 of 10") to screen readers (`aria-describedby` on the article pointing at the counter). (open; screen readers only)
- Quiz tab: the "Preparing" / "Making cards" chips change silently; add a polite live region. (open; screen readers only)
- Copy repeats: "Needs 2+ cards" next to "Only 1 flashcard"; "You got every question right." under a 10 of 10 score; a correct True answer repeats the statement; drop the exclamation in "New best!". (fixed in 7A: one card says "Only 1 flashcard. A quiz needs 2." with no second label; the all-right line is gone; a correct True names no answer; "New best.")
